[ VFX WORK ]

3D Model Card Launch

This project centered on a 3D model card concept for the BSN Real basketball brand launch, designed to feel premium, collectible, and visually futuristic. The composition blends 3D product presentation with dynamic motion, sharp lighting, and a polished brand aesthetic that highlights the card as a premium hero object.

I developed the visual direction around a sleek sports-luxury mood, using layered VFX, atmospheric depth, and controlled compositing to give the card a collectible energy. The final sequence brings together 3D form, motion graphics, and high-end product storytelling to create a launch reveal that feels elevated and memorable.
